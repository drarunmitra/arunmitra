I'm Dr. Arun Mitra Peddireddy, Assistant Professor in the Department of Community and Family Medicine at [AIIMS Bibinagar](https://aiimsbibinagar.edu.in/COMMUNITYANDFAMILYMEDICINE.html), Hyderabad. I am a medical doctor and R educator working in health data science. I am a Co-Investigator on the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) DISHA project on non-communicable disease surveillance, and Principal Investigator on intramural projects on reproductive and child health information systems and tuberculosis notifications. I am also a contributing member of the [R Epidemics Consortium (RECon)](https://www.repidemicsconsortium.org/people/).
