I'm Dr. Arun Mitra Peddireddy, Assistant Professor in the Department of Community and Family Medicine at [AIIMS Bibinagar](https://aiimsbibinagar.edu.in/COMMUNITYANDFAMILYMEDICINE.html), Hyderabad. I am a medical doctor and R educator working in health data science. My current projects include intramural studies on reproductive and child health information systems and tuberculosis notification, which I lead, and the ICMR-funded [DISHA](https://iphbengaluru.res.in/projects/cdoh/disha) surveillance of non-communicable diseases, where I am a Co-Investigator.
